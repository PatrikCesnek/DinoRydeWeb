Ride it like a rider.

DinoRyde plays like a motorbike game with a heartbeat.

Gaits are gears

Walk, Halt, Trot, Canter, Gallop, Sprint, in the 1 N 2 3 4 5 pattern.

Lean into the bends

Tilt your iPhone or iPad to steer, or switch to dragging in Settings.

Watch the breath

Hold a mount at its top gait for too long and it collapses.

Hop the logs

Fallen trunks lie across the whole trail.

Three ways to fall

Slip, get thrown, or rear right over.

Your view, your comfort

Switch off the running head bob in Settings.

Every way to hold the reins

iPhone and iPad

Right pad to go and rein in, left pad to change gait, tilt to steer.

Mac keyboard

W S A D Q E Space

W to go, S to rein in, A and D to lean, Q and E to change gait, Space to hop.

Game controller

Any MFi, Xbox or PlayStation controller works on every device.
